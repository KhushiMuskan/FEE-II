Famous Places

Landmark

Gateway of India

The iconic 26-metre arch built in 1924 overlooking the Arabian Sea — Mumbai's most beloved landmark.

🤍
Promenade

Marine Drive

Mumbai's glamorous seafront boulevard — a 3.6 km arc of Art Deco buildings known as the Queen's Necklace at night.

🤍
Beach

Juhu Beach

Bollywood's favourite stretch of sand — come for street food, sunsets and the chance to spot a film star.

🤍
Heritage

Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus

A UNESCO World Heritage Victorian-Gothic railway station and Mumbai's most photographed building.

🤍
Island

Elephanta Caves

Ancient rock-cut temples on an island accessible by ferry, famous for the majestic Trimurti sculpture.

🤍

Street Food

Pav Bhaji

A buttery mixed-vegetable mash served with toasted bread rolls — the king of Mumbai street food.

Vada Pav

Mumbai's beloved street burger — a spiced potato fritter inside a soft bun with chutneys.

Bhel Puri

Puffed rice tossed with tamarind chutney, onions and coriander — best enjoyed at Chowpatty Beach.

Keema Pav

Spiced minced meat curry served with soft bread rolls — a Colaba classic since the 1950s.
