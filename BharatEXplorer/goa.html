Famous Places

Beach

Baga Beach

One of Goa's most popular beaches — buzzing with beach shacks, water sports and legendary sunsets.

🤍
Waterfall

Dudhsagar Falls

A milky four-tiered waterfall plunging 310 m into a lush forest pool.

🤍
Heritage

Aguada Fort

A 17th-century Portuguese fort perched at the confluence of the Mandovi and the Arabian Sea.

🤍
Beach

Palolem Beach

A crescent-shaped quiet beach in South Goa, perfect for kayaking and dolphin spotting.

🤍
Church

Basilica of Bom Jesus

A UNESCO World Heritage church housing the remains of St. Francis Xavier.

Local Food

Fish Curry Rice

The soul of Goan cuisine — tangy kokum-based curry with fresh catch, served over steamed rice.

Prawn Balchão

A fiery tangy prawn pickle that is unmistakably Portuguese-Goan in origin.

Bebinca

Goa's famous layered dessert made from coconut milk and eggs — a celebration on a plate.

Goan Sausages

Spiced pork sausages with a smoky, tangy kick — best enjoyed with pão bread.
