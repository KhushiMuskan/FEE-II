The City Beautiful

Designed by Le Corbusier, Chandigarh blends modernist architecture with lush green boulevards and a serene lakeside.

Famous Places

Landmark

Rock Garden

Nek Chand's extraordinary 40-acre sculpture park crafted entirely from industrial and urban waste materials.

🤍
Nature

Sukhna Lake

A man-made reservoir at the foothills of the Himalayas — perfect for morning walks and paddleboating.

🤍
Garden

Rose Garden

Asia's largest rose garden covering 30 acres with over 1600 species — spectacular in February.

🤍
Architecture

Capitol Complex

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — Le Corbusier's masterpiece of modernist civic architecture.

🤍
Leisure

Leisure Valley

A 8 km continuous green belt of themed gardens running through the heart of the city.

🤍

Local Food

Stuffed Paratha

Flaky whole-wheat bread stuffed with spiced potato or paneer, served with white butter and pickle.

Chole Bhature

Spiced chickpea curry paired with fluffy deep-fried bread — a Chandigarh breakfast staple.

Punjabi Lassi

Thick, creamy yogurt drink topped with a dollop of homemade butter — best at Sector 17.

Butter Chicken

Creamy tomato-based chicken curry — one of Punjab's most iconic and beloved dishes.
